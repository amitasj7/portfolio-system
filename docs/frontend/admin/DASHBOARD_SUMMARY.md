# 🥇 TYPE 1 — ACTION-DRIVEN DASHBOARD (Most Common)
Industry Purpose

Used when:

Opportunities are time-sensitive

Human action matters more than trends

Used by: internal tools, CRMs, hiring dashboards, admin panels

What It Answers

“What should I do right now?”

What to Show (Top → Bottom)

New / unread leads

High-priority leads (Recruiters)

Pending actions

Quick shortcuts

Why This Is Perfect for YOU

Portfolio = job opportunities

Leads = highest ROI

Matches your earlier decisions

📄 Markdown Design Spec (Type 1)
## Dashboard Type: Action-Driven

### Primary Goal
Surface items that require immediate admin attention.

---

###  Top Section: Attention Alerts
- New leads count (today / unread)
- Recruiter leads highlighted
- Warning if leads are unattended for X days

---

### Middle Section: Quick Actions
- View new leads
- Reply to last recruiter lead
- Update profile
- Add new project

---

### Bottom Section: Recent Activity
- Recently updated projects
- Last profile update date

---

### Design Principles
- Minimal charts
- Text-first signals
- Clear priority hierarchy

---

### KPI Philosophy
Speed > Depth

---

### Best For
- Solo founders
- Job seekers
- Freelancers
- Admin-only tools


# 🥈 TYPE 2 — PERFORMANCE SNAPSHOT DASHBOARD
Industry Purpose

Used when:

You want a health overview

Decisions are periodic, not urgent

Used by: SaaS dashboards, management views

What It Answers

“How am I doing overall?”

What to Show

Lead trends

Conversion indicators

Monthly summaries

When YOU Would Use This

Later, when:

You have consistent traffic

You care about trends, not just alerts

📄 Markdown Design Spec (Type 2)
## Dashboard Type: Performance Snapshot

### Primary Goal
Provide a high-level health overview of the system.

---

### Top Section: KPI Cards
- Total leads this month
- Recruiter leads percentage
- Profile views (if available)

---

### Middle Section: Trends
- Leads per week/month
- Recruiter vs Other leads comparison

---

### Bottom Section: System Health
- Profile completeness status
- Resume last updated date

---

### Design Principles
- Calm visuals
- Aggregated data
- No urgency bias

---

### KPI Philosophy
Clarity > Speed

---

###  Best For
- Mature products
- Weekly reviews
- Management perspective

🔝 TOP LEAD TYPES (INDUSTRY STANDARD)
1️⃣ Recruiter / Talent Acquisition ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (Highest Priority)

Who they are
Internal recruiters
Agency recruiters
Talent partners
Why highest priority
Direct hiring intent
Time-sensitive
Clear next steps (screening, interview)
Typical message
“We’re hiring for X role. Can we talk?”

2️⃣ Founder / Co-founder ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Who they are
Startup founders
CTOs
Early-stage decision makers
Why important
Hiring + freelance + long-term roles
High influence, fewer layers
Typical message

“We’re building X and need help with Y.”

3️⃣ Hiring Manager / Team Lead ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Who they are
Engineering managers
Product leads
Team heads
Why important
Strong signal of serious hiring
Often comes after recruiter or directly
Typical message

“I lead the frontend team and liked your work.”

4️⃣ Client / Business Inquiry ⭐⭐⭐
Who they are
Freelance clients
Agencies
Small businesses
Why medium priority
Revenue opportunity
Less career leverage than hiring roles
Typical message

“Can you build / consult on X?”

5️⃣ Investor / Advisor ⭐⭐
Who they are
Angel investors
Mentors
Advisors
Why lower (for now)
Rare
Long-term value, not immediate
Typical message

“Let’s talk about your work / ideas.”

6️⃣ Community / Collaboration ⭐⭐
Who they are
Open-source contributors
Event organizers
Content collaborators
Why lower
Networking value
No immediate job or money

Typical message

“Let’s collaborate on a project / talk.”

7️⃣ Other / Unknown ⭐
Who they are
Unclear intent
Spam-ish but human
Students, general queries
Why lowest
No clear outcome
Still should be readable
